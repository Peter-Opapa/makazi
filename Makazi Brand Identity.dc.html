02 — LOGO SYSTEM

One mark, built from blocks.

The Makazi mark is an isometric “M” monogram assembled from modular blocks — individual units stacking and connecting into a single structure. It reads as digital infrastructure, growth and connection, never a house. Abstract, geometric, and legible from favicon to signage.

PRIMARY VERTICAL LOCKUP
THE ISOMETRIC M
SYMBOLISM

Stacked, interlocking isometric blocks. Each block is a unit, a home, a record; together they assemble into one connected structure — the operating system beneath rental housing.

WHY IT FITS

Landlords manage 5–500 units; the mark literally stacks them. The 3-D build signals technology and infrastructure, the upward assembly signals growth, and the interlock signals connection — all without a roof, key or house.

HOW IT SCALES

Mark alone for app icon and favicon; vertical lockup for website, deck and print; horizontal lockup for product chrome and signage. Solid green stays crisp small; reversed to a single light tone on ink and green fields.

STRENGTHS

Distinctive ownable “M”, rich infrastructure story, premium isometric craft.

CARE

Keep line weights even; below ~20px use a simplified single-tone cut.

CLEARSPACE

Clear margin = the width of one block on every side. Never crowd the mark.

05 — BRAND SYSTEM

The rules that make it feel like one thing.

A shared toolkit for spacing, icons, illustration, photography, buttons and layout — so every touchpoint, from an SMS to an investor deck, reads unmistakably Makazi.

Spacing — the 8-point grid

Everything spaces in multiples of 8 (4 for fine detail). Radii: 8px controls, 16px cards, 24px surfaces, 99px pills.

Icon style

1.7px monoline, rounded joins, 24px grid, generous counters. Never filled, never duotone. The same clean, geometric logic as the mark.

Illustration style

Flat geometric compositions built from the logo's isometric blocks and modular units. Two colours max per scene. No characters, no gradients.

Photography direction

real Kenyan landlords · natural light

Documentary, warm daylight, real people and real estates — not stock skyscrapers. Faces of the actual users: landlords, caretakers, tenants. Muted, filmic grade that sits beside the green.

Button style

Flat fills, 10px radius, no shadow. One accent button per screen, maximum.

Dashboard & marketing tone

Dashboard — Calm, dense-but-airy, hairline dividers instead of boxes-in-boxes, data-first, money in mono. Linear's structure, Notion's ease.

Marketing — Confident whitespace, one idea per screen, big type, one product truth at a time. Stripe-grade restraint with Kenyan warmth.

★ Flagship feature

Makazi Rental Passport

Every payment, lease and move quietly builds a tenant’s verified rental history — from day one, whether they open the app or not. Think LinkedIn for renting: a portable, Makazi-verified record of trust.

Over years, this longitudinal data becomes the foundation for alternative credit scoring, rent advances and deposit financing — surfaced to partners only with the tenant’s explicit consent. It is difficult for new entrants to replicate, and one of Makazi’s strongest long-term moats.

WHAT IT NEVER STORES (YET)

No credit score. No loan eligibility. No lending decisions. Just a verified, consent-based history. Trust first — finance comes later.
